What to build (REMOTE section 5.7, the paragraph 'A capture is N >= 1 posts' and the four rules after it). Three additive wire paths, one edition, no PROTOCOL bump: (1) request/complete accepts `partial: {stdout, stderr}` in place of `capture`: append to the slot, the run is not over; the post carrying `capture` ends it and its text is what was not yet posted. (2) request/capture accepts `since: {stdout: n, stderr: m}` (byte counts the asker holds); the answer then carries text from those offsets, under `partial` on reply/routed while live and under `capture` when over; without `since` the read is unchanged. (3) reply/routed gains `partial`, whose presence (empty included) says a machine has posted for the slot. Slot rules: every capture poll stamps the slot; a post for a slot nobody polled in thirty seconds, or already collected or swept, is refused in band; a slot holding a post is never handed out again: if its client asks for work again, or thirty seconds pass with no post, the server answers it in doubt with the existing doubt sentence plus how many bytes had arrived (evaluated lazily at the next read or poll, no timer thread); an unacknowledged slot keeps the three hand-offs. The server drops text an asker's `since` has passed and bounds unread text per slot at the old capture limit, noting the drop on stderr. Regenerate the corpus (make corpus) so the three paths are stamped.

Tested by: src/registry/mailbox/slots tests: posts absorb in order; since is idempotent (asking twice at one offset answers the same text); final capture with since carries only the remainder; a single-post foot behaves exactly as before; acknowledged slot answered in doubt on re-ask and on silence; a post for an unlistened slot refused; unread bound enforced. Codec round-trips in all three serializations; corpus diff test green. A foot-grade peer may still send exactly three verbs.

Order: first. thrall's streaming ball vendors the frames this stamps; the bz-arm ball needs it.
