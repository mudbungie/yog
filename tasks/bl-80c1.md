Operator requirement (2026-09-01, paraphrased): the desktop seat and the android client must have interaction parity — NOT identical placement, but if something is interactable in one client it must exist in the other. Drift between the two surfaces must be caught mechanically going forward, not noticed by hand.

This is a DESIGN ball. Deliverable: a tracked design doc (docs/, edited like code — the ball records the work, the file is the artifact), plus implementation balls filed in the right stores (yog / lernie / yog-android) when the design settles. Do not implement here.

A candidate framing to attack, not a decision: comparing the two clients pairwise is the anti-pattern — components meet at the interface, never pairwise. The boundary's Action roster is already the single source of truth for 'things a client can do', so the meta-test may be: each client proves COVERAGE OF THE ROSTER (via its accessibility tree / registered gestures), and parity falls out as both being judged against the one authority. A new roster entry then automatically reddens whichever client has not surfaced it — which is exactly the drift alarm wanted. Attack this: what does it NOT solve? Known holes to address: client-local interactions that cross no wire (text selection, scrolling, copy) — do they belong in the parity contract at all, and if so what is their one authoritative inventory? Deliberate per-platform absences (a thing that should NOT exist on mobile) — an exemption needs a home that is config/annotation, not code, and must be loud, not silent.

Inputs to read: DESIGN sec 8.5 (control boundary), the boundary roster sources (src/boundary/), REMOTE sec 12 (the seat), the android client repo's surface, and the two sibling harness balls (bl-dc07 in the lernie store, bl-243b in the yog-android store) whose AccessKit / uiautomator dumps are the likely measurement instruments — the parity test should consume those inventories rather than invent a third.

Priorities per the house style: one authoritative home per fact, no index unless the index is the app, exemptions sever as config.
