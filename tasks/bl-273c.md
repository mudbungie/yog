Found by bl-9639's one-time DESIGN sweep (Voussoir, 2026-08-14), slice §12–§16. This is the layer bl-9f72 (closed) did NOT reach: that ball made the map's PATHS an invariant — `tests/design_module_map.rs`, every src file has a row and every row names a live path, both directions. The table states three more rules about itself, and the guard sees none of them.

WHAT THE GUARD ACTUALLY CHECKS (read tests/design_module_map.rs, 168 lines, 4 tests): `every_source_file_has_a_row`, `every_row_names_a_file_that_exists`, `the_sweep_is_not_vacuous` (>100 rows and >100 files), `braces_expand`. It never parses the `Est. lines` column (it splits on `|` and reads field 1 only), never looks at row order, cannot see a duplicated row (`mapped()` returns a BTreeSet), and skips test corpora entirely (`is_test_corpus`), so nothing under `tests/` or `scripts/` is checked in either direction.

THE THREE UNHELD RULES.

1. LINE COUNTS. Of 389 numeric entries across §12 and §12.2, **160 are wrong**; 90 of the 147 numeric rows carry at least one wrong number. 90 are off by 1–5, 23 by 6–10, 23 by 11–25, 13 by 26–50, and 11 by more than 50. Worst: tests/design_citations.rs 160→284, src/layout.rs 118→235, src/boundary/codec/fields.rs 61→164, src/shell/pane.rs 140→211, src/git_tree/model.rs 218→285, src/search/mod.rs 175→240, src/app/echo.rs 195→256, src/git_tree/probe.rs 110→54, src/git_tree/streaming.rs 169→223, src/lib.rs 160→107. The column is headed 'Est. lines', which is honest about being an estimate, but §12's doctrine sentence leans on it: 'anything projected ≥200 is pre-split at design time, not at the cap'. The shell/acceptance rows spell a budget instead of a count — '≤250 each' — and 11 of those files are over it (inbox_composer 297, elision 294, fixture 291, settings 275, name_column 269, walls 268, naming 264, echo 259, unfold/mod 255, legible/mod 255); none breaches the 300 hard cap, which `make line-cap` does enforce.

2. SORT ORDER. §12: 'Rows stay sorted by module path — inserts distribute instead of stacking at subsystem boundaries'. Over 226 rows there are 37 strict descents (29 under the most generous normalisation, allowing a directory's mod.rs first and comparing component-wise), and a longest-increasing-subsequence bound says at least 75 rows must move. Whole subsystems are misplaced: src/alert/ after src/attention/, src/bz_host.rs after src/context/, src/config_edit/ after src/control/, src/shell/pane.rs between src/login/ and src/keymap/, src/git_tree/project.rs after src/workdiff/, src/search/ after src/shell/acceptance/wound.rs, src/theme/mod.rs after src/theme/icon/tests/, src/world/mod.rs after src/world/wall.rs.

3. TEST MODULES. §12: 'a test module is covered by its production module's row and never earns one of its own; the budget below is the production file's'. 24 test-corpus paths carry their own row entry and their own number — src/alert/{mod,send,tests}.rs, src/inspector/{mod,tests/mod,tests/tabs,tests/raw,tests/config,tests/pinned}.rs, src/keymap/{…,tests/*}.rs, src/theme/icon/tests/{mod,geometry,artifacts}.rs, src/composer/recall{.rs,/tests.rs}, src/world/{…,tests}.rs, src/opslog/{…,rows/tests,…}. The guard cannot catch it: it excludes test corpora from the file sweep.

DELIVERABLE. Attack the stored-fact problem first, not the numbers: a line count in a doc is a **second representation of a computable fact**, and 160 wrong entries is what the house rule predicts ('two representations of one fact will drift'; 'don't store what you can compute'). The elegant default is therefore SUBTRACTION — delete the Est. lines column outright and let `make line-cap` (the 300 hard cap) and the pre-split aspiration in prose carry what the numbers pretended to; §12 then stays what it is good at, the responsibility map, which is already machine-checked. If the numbers are kept instead, they must be derived or checked, not hand-typed. Sort order and the test-module rule are cheap the other way: both are 5-line assertions in tests/design_module_map.rs (non-descending first path per row; no row cell naming a test corpus), and a rule the table states about itself with no check is exactly the shape bl-9f72 already ruled against. Also worth closing while in there: a duplicated row is invisible to the guard (the BTreeSet) — bl-b6d0 deleted one that had been sitting there since `wire.rs` landed.
