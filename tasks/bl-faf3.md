The drive, as run. One scratch server of this tree on a loopback port, one workspace, two processes of the published streaming foot (thrall 0.0.28) on hand-carried foot-grade leaves: one advertising only bz, one advertising one ordinary tool. The provider was simulated: the bz entry's command was a script speaking brazen's envelope, so no model was called and no credential existed; the seat was yog gesture. No remote host and no running service was touched.

Observed against REMOTE 5.7: providers and catalog appended the far row qualified, in a quarter second; models ran on the named machine and refused the tool-only one; a role set to the qualified row drove a turn of three model calls on the inference foot whose tool call (loaded through clients, held, released by /answer) ran on the other foot; follow streamed the reply while the far child was still writing; /login on the qualified row was fired headless off that machine's catalog and login-tail showed the device line one second after the act, six before the exit, then outcome 0 and one trail row; a stop mid-stream ended the far child thirty-one seconds later; a suspended inference foot failed the turn in band with the arm's sentence, and on waking ran the stale call until its first post more than thirty seconds after the arm's last poll.
