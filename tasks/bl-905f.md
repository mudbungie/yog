## Operator complaint (2026-08-01, verbatim)

'the inference status at the top is good, but I want to see in-flight call characteristics at the bottom of the screen while it's going. something so that if I'm looking down at the chat, I'll see that it's working.'

The chat is tail-anchored now (bl-5cdb), so the operator's eyes live at the BOTTOM. The top status is out of their field of view while reading the tail. While anything is in flight for the focused conversation, a strip at the bottom of the screen must say so, with live characteristics — so a glance down-screen answers 'is it working?'

## Corrections to this ball's premises (found on implementation)

1. **The tool name was NOT available.** The ball said 'name from the step's tool records', but `git_tree::ToolCall` carried only `tool_id` (`toolu_01abc…`) and `state` — the name lives in `input.json`'s `name` field and nothing read it into the snapshot. Fixed by adding `ToolCall::name: Option<String>`, read in `git_tree/tools.rs` at **enumerate time**, beside the two presence checks that already decide the state (the bl-cad5 pattern the ball asked for). A record with no parsable name drops the segment.
2. **Elapsed is not derivable, and is dropped.** `response.json`'s mtime is when the LAST token landed, not the first; a birth time is neither portable (§10) nor free; and a start recorded when yog first *saw* the call is a fact about yog's history, not the world — the stored-flag shape §5.1 #28 exists to refuse. Recorded in DESIGN §11 and in `flight.rs`'s own doc. The pulse is what says 'still alive'; the counters say how far along.
3. **A 'quiet Ns' counter was considered and rejected** as a stand-in for elapsed: `now - last_action_unix` already has a seat (the list row's age label), and a second one would be two homes for one fact.
