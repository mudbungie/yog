- `boundary::login::…::a_lane_ends_when_the_run_it_was_reading_is_gone` — landed with bl-c285 (the sign-in act); the lane/holder pair rendezvous on a run's settlement, and the beat's wait is the suspect.
- `boundary::consumer::…::the_boot_answers_a_dead_claimants_gesture_in_doubt` — the consumer's boot-time doubt arm.

Same class as bl-b8c8 and the sibling it fixed (`registry::mailbox::slots::tests::work_landing_mid_hold_ends_it`): a wall-clock wait standing in for a cross-thread rendezvous. The fix is the same shape — hand off on something both sides observe (the published state, a channel, the claim the read takes on its way into the hold), with an escape deadline that fails instead of hangs. Do not widen a sleep.
