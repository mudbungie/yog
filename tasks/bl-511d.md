Round 2, lane comparator. yog main 5cd95986, engine on an isolated world.

## The gesture

bl-d118 is fixed: `/revoke` now holds. Verified —

    yog gesture --ws w --agent A '/revoke'
      {"kind":"floored","ok":true,"standing":true}
    yog gesture --ws w --agent A '/message Run this bash command now: `echo X > /tmp/marker`. …'
    yog gesture --ws w --agent A '/nudge'

The transcript's last entry is the unanswered call and the marker file does not exist:

    model | [{"kind":"thinking","text":""},
             {"id":"call_Lqep…","input":"{\"command\":\"echo … > /tmp/marker\"}",
              "kind":"tool-use","name":"bash"}]

`/answer refuse` then names the exact call, which is the good half:

    {"advanced":true,"kind":"answered","ok":true,"tool":"bash",
     "tool_use":"call_Lqep…","verdict":"refuse"}

## The defect

While that call was held, `/attention` said:

    workspace | RubberTunnel | quiescent | held= None | says: came to rest — your turn

`held` is null and the sentence is the ordinary one. The conversation is not at rest — it is parked on an adjudication that only the operator can release, and the attention queue is the one surface whose whole job is to say so. An operator who revokes autonomy and walks away is never told the agent is waiting; the row is indistinguishable from a conversation that finished.

The same field is null and the same sentence appears on a conversation whose BIRTH failed (see the p1 filed by this lane): `state: stopped`, `says: came to rest — your turn; has mail queued and no driver taking it`. Three different conditions — finished, blocked on a human, dead — read as one row.

## Expected

A held call sets `held` on the attention row and changes the sentence: the tool name, and the verb that releases it. Something of the shape "holding `bash` — /answer pass|refuse". A birth or step failure says failed, not rest.

## Severity

p3 as a rendering, but it is the second half of bl-d118: a floor that holds and is not reported is a floor the operator learns about by finding nothing happened.
