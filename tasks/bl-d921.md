Repro (drive harness, default window): press 'w' (bare) — the modal opens. Press Escape once or twice: the modal stays. Click the left panel's Config entry while the modal is open: the config pane toggles UNDER the modal — the click lands on the surface beneath. Industry norm and DESIGN's own focus discipline ('a dismissed modal' parks the keyboard in the composer) both assume a modal can be dismissed by Escape and owns the pointer while open. Fix: Escape closes the modal (drops the draft name); while open, clicks outside it either close it or are consumed — never delivered beneath. Acceptance: a drive beat opens with w, Escapes, asserts the modal gone and no workspace minted; a click at the Config entry's coordinate with modal open does not toggle config.
