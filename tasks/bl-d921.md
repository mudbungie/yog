Repro (drive harness, default window): press 'w' (bare) — the modal opens. Press Escape once or twice: the modal stays. Click the left panel's Config entry while the modal is open: the config pane toggles UNDER the modal — the click lands on the surface beneath. THIRD finding (2026-08-01): with a VALID name typed, Return does not submit the dialog either — the only way through is the pointer on 'Create workspace'. Industry norm for a single-field dialog: Enter submits, Escape cancels, clicks outside don't leak beneath. DESIGN's focus discipline ('a dismissed modal' parks the keyboard in the composer) assumes dismissal exists. Fix all three: Enter submits when the name validates (does nothing when invalid — message already renders), Escape closes dropping the draft, pointer input outside the modal is consumed. Acceptance: drive beats assert w→type→Return mints and focuses the sphere; w→Escape leaves no modal and no mint; a click at Config's coordinate with the modal open toggles nothing.
