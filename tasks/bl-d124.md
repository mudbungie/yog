Found from lernie's stress test (lernie bl-5439). Gesture: yog fixture busy (0.0.76) on an isolated port, boot the engine on that root, then 'lernie attention' (or any client's attention op). Expected: the queue's five rows in well under a second, as workspaces, ops, board and transcript answer. Observed: no reply after 20 s, every time, from the first request on a fresh engine; the engine's side of the socket sits in CLOSE-WAIT after the client gives up. Likely the attention fold blocks on something the fixture's hold paths imply (the inbox executor lock or a response.json writer) rather than reading it as a live call. Separately noticed: the same fixture engine's board and balls ops list ball stores from outside the fixture root, so the isolated world is not isolated for balls.
