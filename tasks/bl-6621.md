`budgets::BudgetSpend::total_tokens` sums all four brazen `Usage` counters,
and `spend::prices::Rates::price` charges each of the four at its own
per-million rate:

    rate(spend.input_tokens,       self.input)
      + rate(spend.output_tokens,     self.output)
      + rate(spend.cache_read_tokens, self.cache_read)
      + rate(spend.cache_write_tokens,self.cache_write)

Both are correct only where the four counters are **disjoint slices** of
one prompt. DESIGN §12 row 35 already knows they are not, and says so in
yog's own words — it is the reason the context-fullness figure uses
`max(input, cache_read + cache_write)`:

  "Anthropic reports the three as **disjoint** slices of one prompt while
  OpenAI's `prompt_tokens` and Google's `promptTokenCount` already
  **contain** the cached slice beside them"

So on an OpenAI- or Google-shaped provider row the cached tokens are
counted once inside `input_tokens` and again in `cache_read_tokens`. The
token figure inflates, and the dollar figure inflates twice over: the
same tokens are billed at the input rate AND at the cache-read rate.
Downstream, `spend::ceiling` gates spawns on that dollar figure, so an
operator's ceiling refuses births earlier than the number they typed.

Measured on a live conversation tree on an `openai-chatgpt` row (24 steps
across a root and 8 subagents): the summed figure is 2,248,984 tokens
where the overlap-corrected figure is 1,796,888 — 452,096 phantom tokens,
+25%, and rising with cache hit rate. One step's usage line, verbatim:

    {"type":"usage","input_tokens":93556,"output_tokens":132,
     "cache_read_tokens":91648,"cache_write_tokens":null}

93,556 prompt tokens billed as 185,336.

**This is a faithful mirror, not a yog invention, and that is the
constraint on fixing it.** `budgets/mod.rs` sums four *deliberately* —
"mirrors lernie's own `budget::spend` derivation exactly … so the figure
yog shows is the figure that exhausts `max_total_tokens`" — and that
promise is the whole point of the module. Correcting the fold here alone
would make yog's figure stop matching the ceiling it is a preview of.

So this is **downstream of lernie bl-68f5**, which carries the same
finding against `prompt/budget/derive.rs::usage_tokens` and proposes the
two candidate shapes (normalize the overlap in brazen's decoders, or fold
defensively as `max(input, cache_read + cache_write) + output`). Whichever
lands, yog's move is the same and small: keep mirroring, and add a
regression that pins one OpenAI-shaped `Usage` line to a non-doubled
token count and a non-doubled price.

The pricing half is separable and arguably wrong on its own terms even
today — a rate table with distinct `input` and `cache_read` rates is
describing disjoint slices by construction, so feeding it overlapping
ones misprices regardless of what any ceiling does. But splitting the fix
across the two crates would give the pane a dollar figure derived one way
and a token figure derived another, which is worse than being wrong
consistently. Land it with lernie's.
