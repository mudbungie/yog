DESIGN §4.1, REMOTE §7. Found by the §11-rule sweep (bl-7dca); the same
defect bl-b986 just closed for `pinned`, one document over.

`UiState` keeps two documents (bl-8bbc): the shared world `ui.json`, and one
**pane** document per registered client at
`<state-root>/clients/<client>/pane.json` holding `collapsed`, `panels`,
`zoom`, `transcript_expand_responses`, `transcript_expand_others` and
`notify_unfocused`.

**Not one of those six has a reader or a writer outside `src/ui_state` and its
own tests.** No `Action` writes one, no `Reply` carries one. Their only callers
were the frame's (`set_zoom` off the zoom keys, `set_panel_size` off a released
drag, `is_collapsed` off a section header, the fold knobs off the transcript),
and bl-7942 deleted the frame. So yog stores per-seat glass facts, keeps two
documents and a per-client path derivation to hold them, and no seat can read or
set any of them.

`identity_last_used` is the same defect in the world document with one half
still standing: `AppModel` READS it (`app::balls`, the `--as` fallback for
verbs dispatched outside a workspace) and `set_identity` has no caller at all.
So the fallback is permanently `$USER`, and §4.1's "prefills `--as` … default
`$USER` when absent" is true only because nothing can ever make it non-absent.

Why it matters rather than being dead weight: §4.1 argues each of these keys is
*durable* precisely so it converges across a client's seats — "a fold answered
on the phone must clear on the desktop". That promise is unkept, and a seat
that stores its own zoom locally instead is the drift the two-document split
exists to prevent.

Shape questions, unanswered here:
- One `Action::SetPane`/`Query::Pane` over the whole document (it is one
  client's bag of glass facts, and a per-key verb would be six), against
  §8.5's own rule that a view gains no boundary representation — which bl-b986
  has just shown is not absolute where the fact is *durable and converging*.
- Or the pane document is a mistake and these belong in each seat's own local
  storage, in which case `src/ui_state`'s second `Doc`, `registry::pane`,
  `registry::PANE` and `ui_state::panels` all delete, and §4.1 loses a block.
- `notify_unfocused` additionally gates a behaviour that reaches nothing
  (bl-09ef), so it should be decided with that ball.
- `identity_last_used` needs either a writer or the honest statement that the
  fallback is `$USER` and the key is vestigial.

Whichever lands, §4.1 must be amended: today it documents six keys as live
policy.
