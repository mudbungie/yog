Operator report 2026-08-21: a wall with a large conversation list cannot be
deleted through the UI — the list pushes the rest of the navigator column off
the pane, taking the delete door with it. Two stacked layout defects, both
verified; the delete verbs themselves are sound (the §8.5 lines
/delete-workspace and /delete-agent work throughout).

## A — the navigator column has no vertical budget

- `src/shell/conv_list.rs:79` — the conversation list is
  `egui::ScrollArea::vertical()` with no max_height, FIRST in the plain
  top-down stack of `src/shell/navigator.rs:21-51`. egui sizes a ScrollArea
  from available_rect_before_wrap and only shrinks it when content is smaller,
  so a list taller than the panel consumes the whole column.
- Everything declared after it — the separator (:29), balls section (:30),
  clients (:35), and the ⚙ Config / Login entries (:42-51) — lays out past the
  panel bottom and is clipped by egui's panel clip (invisible AND
  un-clickable). ⚙ Config is the only visible door to the workspace danger row
  (`src/shell/delete.rs:68-89`, seated at `src/shell/config_edit/mod.rs:177`).
- DESIGN promises the opposite: §11 rule 6 (DESIGN:6418-6432) bounded-viewport
  scrolling "in the room it was left rather than growing the panel or painting
  over what is docked beneath it", and DESIGN:5809-5813 claims the unfold
  scrolls in its room. Rule 5's budget machinery (`layout::share`/`ROW`,
  `src/layout.rs:50-93`) exists and is applied only to the center pane's
  docked accessories — the navigator column never calls it.
- Fix shape (implementer decides): either give the list a bounded share via
  the existing rule-5 machinery, or seat the tail entries
  (balls/clients/Config/Login) as a bottom band inside the side panel so they
  claim first, mirroring rule 5's "the band at the container's bottom edge
  claims first". Prefer the existing mechanism over a new one.

## B — both delete dialogs enumerate unbounded, pushing their own fire button off-screen

- `src/shell/delete.rs:132-136` — the workspace dialog window is
  .collapsible(false).resizable(false), no ScrollArea, no max_height; the body
  (:145-193) prints one line per dying conversation with no cap, and the
  arming TextEdit (:164) + Delete button (:176) are declared AFTER the
  enumeration — enough conversations put them past the screen bottom.
- `src/shell/delete_agent.rs:174-181` vs :194-224 — identical shape for the
  descendant census of a single-conversation delete.
- §3.6 (DESIGN:1113-1115) mandates the concrete enumeration; nothing bounds
  its height. Fix shape: wrap each enumeration in
  ScrollArea::vertical().max_height(…) (the idiom config_edit/mod.rs:162 and
  shell/settings.rs:81 already use); the arming row and fire button stay
  outside/below the scrolled census so their seat cannot move.
