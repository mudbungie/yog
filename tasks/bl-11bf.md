Replace the mark landed by bl-4c63/bl-70a8 (DESIGN §11).

**the operator's critique:** the old mark read as a jester's hat, and three things
conspired — purple/gold/green IS the carnival tricolor; the arms bulged and
ended fat at the rim like hat points rather than tapering; and twelve beads on
the rim read as jingle bells (and were pure noise at 64 px). Three directions
were prototyped; the operator chose **circuit triskele**: "the circuit looks really
good. The only thing is that the eye in the middle has underlap from the arms.
Probably want explicit black in the middle."

**What lands:**
- Two hues, both derived from HYDRA — phosphor conductor and a dim casing under
  it. No gold; the carnival tax is what the third saturated hue was paying.
- Arms are **arc-only**, built by compass: an arc is named by two endpoints and
  a sagitta, so the construction is a pair of circles, not a tuned spline.
- Nodes on the wire replace beads on the rim: two an arm, not four.
- The eye's pupil is a **lune** — the region between two arcs on one pair of
  cusps — filled with the theme's own `VOID_DEEP`. Punching it as negative
  space let the traces show through from underneath (the operator's note), and an
  opaque fill also spares the renderers a coverage-subtraction primitive
  neither has.

**Constraints:** two flat-fill primitives only (`Band`, `Disc`), so `raster.rs`
and `vector.rs` are untouched; `assets/yog.svg` regenerated by `make icon`; it
must survive 64 px; 100% coverage; 300-line cap.
