Found during bl-6191 (see its journal): AppModel::last_failure() is one global query over the ops tail with no surface attribution, and three surfaces render it unconditionally every frame — src/shell/input_bar.rs (composer), src/shell/start_rows.rs (balls fold), src/shell/bootstrap.rs. The composer correctly banners a start failure; the balls-fold copy is the same fact painted twice on a surface that didn't originate it. DESIGN §7.3 says 'the originating surface renders the failure' — the code cannot honour that because SurfaceFailure carries argv/stderr and no origin. Fix: attribute failures to their origin in the ops-row projection (an origin field derived at dispatch, not stored beyond the trail), and each surface renders only its own; or amend §7.3 if one-banner-everywhere is actually wanted. Verify against current main first — bl-6191 (3d6e296) touched the neighbouring code.
