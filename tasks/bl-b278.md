## Why this is a defect and not a number

The cap is meant to fire at design time, on the author about to add to a file —
CLAUDE.md is explicit that ≥200 is *"a design-time projection rule"* and that
the remedy is *"split along a real seam (DESIGN §12) — do not shave lines"*. A
file resting at 300 inverts that: the rule fires on whoever happens to touch it
next, at the moment they are finishing something else, and the cheapest way out
under that pressure is exactly the shave the rule forbids. One agent already
reported trimming a doc comment to stay under the wall this week — the count
turned out to be wrong, so nothing was actually shaved, but the reflex is the
one this ball exists to remove.

Two of the three are `src/shell/acceptance/*`, which is the surface the
remaining REMOTE §8.2 window chain (bl-028a, bl-670c, bl-f29c) must add
acceptance coverage to. They will hit this.
