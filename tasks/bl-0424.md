Operator reports 2026-08-21, one root cause, two symptoms:
(a) at half-screen window width a narrow semi-transparent vertical bar
flickers at the seam between the navigator column and the center pane;
(b) the navigator column cannot be dragged below ~half the window — the
boundary slides back out.

## Verified mechanism

egui stores a SidePanel's CONTENT rect as next frame's width and re-clamps
into width_range every frame (egui 0.29 panel.rs:287/332/243). The roster
panel's ceiling is half the window (`layout.rs:57,128-130` KEEP=0.5 via
`ui_state/panels.rs:77-79`, wired `src/shell/mod.rs:182-192`). Sidebar content
whose natural width exceeds the ceiling therefore:
- ratchets a dragged boundary back out (~15 pt/frame, the walk the operator
  sees as "slides back"), until the panel sits pinned at width_range.max —
  symptom (b), and DESIGN:6208-6214 (bl-9669, "The panel's width is the
  operator's, not its content's") already measured and outlawed exactly this;
- when the ceiling clamps panel_rect below the content width, egui clips the
  panel's fill at panel_rect but starts the CentralPanel at the CONTENT
  rect's right edge (panel.rs:279 vs :291/:392) — the interval between them is
  painted by NOBODY and shows eframe's default clear color
  rgba(12,12,12,180) ≈ 70% alpha (eframe epi.rs:193; src/main.rs:265-286
  never overrides clear_color) — symptom (a). It flickers because the content
  width changes frame to frame (live badges/age/flight chips,
  `src/shell/conv_row.rs:138-181`; wire sections materializing,
  `src/shell/wire.rs:95-112`) under the continuous repaint pump.

## Width drivers in the sidebar (ranked; egui clamps TextEdit wrap to
available width, so the two bare TextEdits are NOT drivers)

1. `src/spend/render.rs:32-57` reached from `src/shell/board/rows.rs:84-101`
   (spend: and epic: rows): a five-item bare `ui.horizontal` with a greedy
   Truncate run in the middle and three items laid AFTER it — the rule-1b
   inversion (DESIGN:6268-6272: control first, `shell::row::control_last` is
   "the only lawful spelling"). Natural width ~750-800 pt in a 260 pt column.
   Written for the wide center settings seat (`src/shell/settings.rs:114,124`)
   — the fix must keep that seat correct.
2. `egui::CollapsingHeader` at `src/shell/start_rows.rs:208` and
   `src/shell/board.rs:132` hard-codes TextWrapMode::Extend and never clamps
   desired_width (egui collapsing_header.rs:518-536) — a ~300-350 pt content
   floor (bounded today only by the 32-char elision, bl-ac3d's fix).
3. `src/shell/conv_row.rs:189-225`: `ui.weak(subtitle)` laid AFTER the greedy
   `selectable_label(title)` — another rule-1b inversion; overlaps the age
   column and keeps the row from ever fitting.

## Missing guards (both halves of the fix)

- No width-axis analogue of `pin_to_panel` on the side panel
  (`src/shell/mod.rs:98-100` is height-only), and no `row::shown_width(ui)`
  inside the roster ScrollArea (`src/shell/conv_list.rs:79`; compare
  `src/shell/pane.rs:206`) — ScrollArea::vertical follows its content's width
  outward (egui scroll_area.rs:899-910).
- No `clear_color` override: implement
  `fn clear_color(&self, v: &egui::Visuals) -> [f32;4] {
  v.panel_fill.to_normalized_gamma_f32() }` on the App impl
  (`src/main.rs:265`) so any residual unpainted pixel is opaque panel ground,
  not translucent near-black. (main.rs is coverage-exempt.)

## The change

1. Contain the content: fix the spend/epic row shape (rows on their own
   lines, or through `row::control_last`/`row::peers`), fix the conv_row
   subtitle inversion, and add the panel-level width guards (`row::shown_width`
   at conv_list.rs:79 + a width pin at the side panel root) so no future row
   can ratchet the panel again. The CollapsingHeader floor is acceptable once
   the guards clamp its propagation; do not fork egui.
2. Mask the seam: the clear_color override above.
3. DESIGN §11 rules 1/1b/2/5 (6208-6272, 6339-6379) already promise all of
   this — the fix delivers the doc; amend only where mechanics change (e.g.
   note the clear-color ground and the roster's shown_width), citing this
   ball.
