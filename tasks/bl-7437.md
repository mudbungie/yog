Redesign the application icon (DESIGN §11, `src/theme/icon.rs`).

**Ask (verbatim from the operator):** "I need a logo for yog. It's based on yog-sothoth, because of all the balls that we have. I'd like it to be geometric, art deco. I think three tentacles, with glowing orbs on them, in the shape of a triskelion. The pieces should be thick, so that they are apparent even when reduced to the size of an icon, and they should be generable from code."

**Constraints that survive the redesign:** one geometry table is the single
source of truth; `rgba()` rasterizes it arithmetically (no image decoder, no
new dependency) and `svg()` emits the same geometry as vector source;
`assets/yog.svg` stays a checked-in derivation with a test asserting equality;
transparency, no background plate; must read at 16 px.

**Deliverable:** new geometry in `src/theme/icon.rs`, regenerated
`assets/yog.svg`, DESIGN §11 rewritten to describe what the mark now is.
