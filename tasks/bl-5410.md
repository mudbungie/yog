Found by Larkspur while sweeping bl-36c3 (re-audit of paint-layer assertions). **Measured, not read** — the evidence is `src/shell/acceptance/legible.rs`'s predicate run over the shipped frame, at `df228b0`, which is bl-b531's own delivery. **This is what survives that fix.**

## The predicate that found it

`paint_probe::seen_of` hands back, per galley, the laid-out glyphs, the rect they were laid into, and the part of that rect the clip let through. A run whose **shown width is narrower than its laid width** is one the operator reads the front of and no more — and because the galley was never *truncated*, egui never added the `…` that would say so. That is QUALITY §1 **G1**'s defect exactly: not that text is cut, but that it is cut **silently**.

## Root cause, one line

DESIGN §11 rule 1 (`TextWrapMode::Truncate`) is set at the **side panel's** root (`src/shell/navigator.rs:33`), the flight strip (`flight_strip.rs:47`), the inbox queue (`inbox_queue.rs:130`) and inside `row::control_last`. **Nothing sets it on the centre**, the top bar or the activity panel — egui's default there is `Extend`. So every horizontal label in those seats is laid at its natural width and sliced at the container's edge, mid-glyph, with no marker. Rule 1 is a panel rule that reads like a window rule, which is why every cut below is in the centre. Recorded in DESIGN §11 as rule 1c under bl-36c3.

## The residue at 420x320, after bl-b531

```
"Stop" laid 25 pt, 16 pt shown                                    (composer verb row)
"(in 10 · out 5 · cache r 50000 · w 0)" laid 189 pt,  79 pt shown  (altitude-1 headline)
"(50000 / 200000 tok · m)"             laid 136 pt, 108 pt shown  (altitude-1 headline)
"auth api_key · <state>"  laid 190 pt, 123 pt shown  (§8.3 provider rows)
"auth bearer · no credential stored"   laid 186 pt,  74 pt shown
"auth none · no credential needed"     laid 183 pt, 109 pt shown
"auth none…"                           laid  68 pt,  36 pt shown
```

`Stop` is a **control** (`src/shell/verb_row.rs:208`), cut to 16 of its 25 points. §11 rule 1b keeps a control from being *elided* by the greedy text beside it; nothing keeps it from being *clipped away* by its seat, and the two are the same loss to the operator.

**`auth none…` is worse than an unmarked cut.** The galley WAS truncated — egui added the `…` to say the row was cut — and the clip then took the ellipsis off too. The marker that exists to prove elision is itself elided, so nothing on the glass says anything is missing. Everything a G1 assertion could look for is gone.

## The gate that now holds it

`src/shell/acceptance/legible.rs::nothing_is_cut_without_an_ellipsis_at_any_window_size` sweeps the whole window at four sizes × trail open/closed × four centre tabs; its sibling does the six inspector tabs. **One-directional**: no run may be cut that is not on a `KNOWN` list, each entry naming the ball that owns it. Every entry names **this ball** — so closing it means deleting them, and the list stays honest by construction. The test will not tell you an entry is stale; leaving one silently re-excuses the defect for the next surface that reproduces it.

Proved to bite: a planted wide label in the top bar reported

    "MUTANT a planted run far wider than the top bar can show at a small window"
    laid 428 pt, 412 pt shown — at 420x320

and **no other test in the tree noticed** (1874 of 1876 green).

## Fix shape (a lead, not a prescription)

The severable answer is to state rule 1 where it is true rather than where it is inherited — the move `row::control_last` already made for rule 1b (bl-bc06's second shape: *"the helper states the whole rule itself and depends on no ambient state"*). Attack it first: a blanket `Truncate` on the centre would also truncate every **control** there, which is the defect rule 1b exists to stop. So it is not one line.
