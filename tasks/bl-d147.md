Found from lernie bl-11da on 2026-10-08, measured on the builder there: with a tarpaulin.toml present, tarpaulin 0.35.2's Config::merge never carries the command-line engine across, and the file's Linux default is ptrace. The Makefile's `--engine llvm` has therefore never taken effect. Consequences seen in lernie: a dependency's same-named relative source path collides with ours under the ptrace loader (phantom uncovered lines), the run is 2x slower, and llvm counts untaken let-else branches ptrace never saw, so switching engines first costs a round of covering those. Fix: `engine = "Llvm"` in tarpaulin.toml with the reason beside it, remove the flag from the Makefile (one home for the engine), cover whatever llvm reports, verify with a builder run.
