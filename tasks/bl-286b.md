## The stale claim

bl-bf35 landed a quiescence-gated restart and justified it, in prose that is now
in the tree, with this:

> a `lernie` turn killed between a tool call and its result leaves an unpaired
> tool-use tail, and every later message on that conversation is refused by the
> provider. That is not a crash the next boot repairs; it is a wedged
> conversation.

The second sentence is **false against the pinned substrate**. lernie `=0.0.11`
carries crash settlement (upstream bl-4187, consumed here in bl-4c1f): at the
next drive boundary, before delivery, every unanswered `tool_use` in the
trailing assistant window is settled with an in-band `is_error` `tool_result`.
It is idempotent — answered ids are read back from the transcript — and it is
covered upstream by a test whose subject is exactly *a deposit revives a crashed
window through settlement*. A killed turn therefore revives on the next deposit.

The graceful path was already covered separately: lernie installs a SIGTERM
handler and the step loop settles its own unanswered window on the way out.

## Why the mechanism still stands

Do not delete the deferral. It is still the right behaviour, for a reason that
is true: a killed turn **discards in-flight work and the spend that bought it**,
and settlement makes the conversation usable again, not whole. The point of
this ball is that a mechanism defended by a false claim is one audit away from
being removed by someone who checks the claim — and equally, a false claim is
how a *worse* mechanism gets kept. Correct the reason, keep the behaviour.

## The residual, which is a different shape

One case is genuinely unrepaired upstream: the **buried** orphan — a user-role
entry already written behind the unpaired `tool_use`. Settlement bails out on
it, and the hop declines, because pairing is positional and cannot be repaired
by appending. Under 0.0.11 that shape can only be debris from before the
settlement landed, since the only runtime delivery site runs strictly after
settlement. yog renders it and repairs nothing: the orphan banner
(`src/steps_view/orphan.rs`, bl-ace6) fires on *newest entry is mail and the
agent lock is free*, and its acceptance fixture is lernie's own decline
sentence.

Two things to settle, and neither is obviously "build something":

1. Whether any live world still carries pre-settlement debris. That is a disk
   question, not a code one — answer it by looking, and if the answer is none,
   record that and move on.
2. Whether the recoverable shape deserves any paint at all. Today a
   markless crashed tail renders nothing — the conversation simply looks idle
   until a deposit revives it, which is correct but silent.

## Scope

Correct the prose wherever bl-bf35 put it — `README.md`'s server-install
section, the header comment and the `decide` commentary in
`scripts/deploy/yog-update`, and the `yog.service` comment that repeats it.
State the true reason (lost work and spend, not a wedge) and cite the
settlement so the next reader does not re-derive this. Verify against the
pinned crate rather than against this body: a ball body is not evidence, and
this one is making exactly that point.
