Left over from bl-b43b, which gave a provider-rung refusal its own §6 word
(`AttentionKind::Refused`), its own hue on the roster row (`Tone::Bad`) and a
`refused` fact on the `agent` answer. The one surface that still says nothing
is the one the operator is actually reading.

## What is still wrong

A conversation refused at its first model call paints the user message and then
nothing at all. `Query::Transcript` answers the committed `messages/` entries
with the in-flight tail folded on, and a refused conversation has one committed
entry and no tail — so the pane is honest and useless. Nothing on it names the
provider row that refused or the act that clears it, and the ops trail's own
`-2` row carries empty stderr because the adapter spoke the failure **in band on
stdout**, into `response.json`, which is where the classification reads it from.

## The shape

The transcript already carries entries no file backs, twice: `EntryKind::
Compacted`, seated into the hole the compactor left, and `EntryKind::Streaming`,
folded on by `Transcript::with_live`. The module doc names them as one move. A
settled-failure notice is that move a third time — a virtual trailing entry
carrying the wound in words.

The vocabulary for those words already exists and is currently dead in
production: `steps_view::Wound` (`None` / `Mute` / `Spoke` / `OutputLimit`),
whose `banner()` composes glyph, class and reason into one sentence, and
`latest_wound`, the conversation-level projection. Both lost their consumer when
the window was severed into a seat. A provider refusal wants a fifth arm there
carrying the provider **row** — which the steps view already routes as
`auth_row` — so the sentence can name the credential to sign in rather than
gesturing at one.

## What it costs, and why it is its own ball

A new `EntryKind` variant, its token in `transcript::wire`, its decoder, and a
corpus regeneration. `reply/transcript` is still at ledger `since: 1` with
PROTOCOL standing at 2, so the signature may move once without a bump — but that
is a shape a seat consumes, and it should move deliberately rather than as a
rider on a derivation fix.

Worth deciding alongside it: whether the notice is the wound generally (a
`Mute`/`Spoke` no-response death is the same class of silent conversation, and
those balls are already on the board) or the refusal alone. The general answer
is almost certainly right and is why this is not a two-line change.
