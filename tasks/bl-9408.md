Measured by bl-5d8d (REMOTE §13.7 ruling 3). From the deployed engine box only dht.transmissionbt.com answers of the bootstrap hosts (two addresses); router.bittorrent.com and router.utorrent.com are silent even to a raw `ping`. That router answers `find_node` with ONE node repeated eight times in its 208-byte `nodes` field (verified over repeated probes), so a walk's first round learns exactly two fresh nodes. When both are silent the walk has nothing unasked in its K closest and ends: `lookup` returns Ok with the two routers as the 'closest nodes' (5 of 20 diagnostic walks ended at 2, 5 or 6 nodes instead of 8; 3 at exactly the routers). A caller that seeds BEP 44 from that answer is dark, because the routers never answer `get` (sibling ball).

To decide: (a) widen `src/wire/rendezvous.rs` `mainline()` — it names two hosts and one of them is silent from the box that carries the wire; candidates are other long-lived bootstrap routers, measured before adding; (b) whether a walk that converged on nothing but bootstrap addresses is an Err (or retries the bootstrap) rather than an Ok the caller cannot tell from success; (c) never return a bootstrap address from `lookup` as a node near the target.
