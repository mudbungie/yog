The Login pane renders ten 'Login <provider>' rows. Live findings (2026-08-01 drive): the dim rows are DISABLED egui buttons — clicking 'Login local' produces nothing, no ops row, no explanation; only openai-chatgpt and claude-session-direct render enabled (presumably the oauth2-auth providers). So: (1) disabled rows give no reason — a disabled affordance must say why ('api-key provider — set the key in config', 'keyless — nothing to log in'); (2) no row shows credential presence — S5 point 4: presence renders. Make presence explicit in words ('signed in' badge / ash absent), not luminance. Acceptance: every row communicates its state (enabled+why, disabled+why, credentialed or not); a keyless row never renders a dead verb.
