Each frame the follow lane emits looks like this:

    {"kind":"follow","ok":true,"stream":{"delta":"text","text":"<…>"}}

`text` is not the increment. It is the **whole accumulated answer so far**, re-sent
from the beginning on every frame. `delta` names the *kind* of stream event, so
nothing in the envelope is lying about its own field — but the word is the first
thing a reader sees and the shape is the opposite of what it promises.

## Measured

One followed answer, driven end to end and captured off the wire:

    frames               32
    final answer         416 bytes
    bytes on the wire  8,310
    amplification       20.0x

Every frame carried the full prefix; the smallest frame's *new* content was a
few characters. The cost is quadratic in the answer's length, so this ratio is
the FLOOR — it was measured on a two-sentence reply. The same frame density over
an eight-hundred-word answer is on the order of half a megabyte on the wire for
five kilobytes of text.

## Why it is this way, and why that is not the end of it

The whole-text frame buys a genuinely valuable property, and DESIGN states it:
"`with_live` **replaces** a live entry rather than appending beside one, so the
pull answer's tail and the follow lane's newer one reconcile by newest-wins and
cannot paint the answer twice". A frame that carries everything is idempotent, is
order-independent, and lets a seat that joined late or dropped a frame be correct
immediately. Against a true delta stream, each of those becomes a case to handle.

That is a real trade and it may well be the right one on loopback. It is a much
worse one for the face this lane exists for: a phone seat, on a mobile link, over
a tailnet, watching a long answer write itself. REMOTE §6 already says a client
holds "key material and RAM" — RAM is exactly what makes newest-wins cheap to
keep locally, and the seat is already holding the accumulated text in order to
paint it.

## What this ball is asking for

Not necessarily a delta stream. Any of these closes the gap, in rising order of
work:

1. **Rename it.** If whole-text is the answer, the field should not be `delta`.
   A reader of the wire — and the next implementer of a seat — should not have
   to measure it to find out.
2. **Coalesce the frames.** 32 frames for 416 bytes is finer than any face can
   paint. A minimum interval or a minimum new-byte count collapses most of the
   amplification without touching the frame's shape or its idempotence.
3. **Send the increment, keep the invariant.** A frame carrying `from` (the byte
   offset it continues at) plus the new text is still reconcilable by
   newest-wins — a seat whose accumulated length does not match `from` asks for a
   pull, which is the mechanism the lane already reconciles against.

(2) alone is most of the win and costs nothing in correctness. Worth deciding
before a second seat implementation exists to keep compatible.
