## The ruling being implemented

DESIGN §3.3 (amended):

> The preamble is therefore **one line and no instruction**: it names the
> sphere the conversation lives in — so an agent reading `bl list` can tell
> its own workspace's claims from a stranger's — and nothing else.
>
>     Your workspace is <name>.

The line previously read `You are <name>.` — the workspace name asserted as
every conversation's identity; N conversations in one sphere all claimed to
*be* the sphere. Nothing else changes: `YOG_NAME`, the `--as` default, and the
§3.2 claimant join all still carry the workspace name; the composer preview
and the stamp remain one string.

Also from §3.3: "**every prefill yog composes leads with its headline** — the
ball rung's first line is `Ball <id>: <title>`, the path rung's is
`Working directory: <dir>` (reworded from a sentence that buried the path on
line two)".
