Rework the icon landed by bl-692f (DESIGN §11).

**the operator's spec, verbatim:** "Make all of the colors a little darker. reduce the
thickness of the gold highlight, but make it constant, not tapering. Make the
tentacles come starting straight out from the base (solves two issues; your top
side of the tentacle doesn't quite connect, and it makes it curve more). The
tentacles should fully flatten by the end, until they merge into a golden ring
of the highlights. They essentially should follow the arc of an orbital
insertion. Finally, the green balls on the tentacles: make them all the same
size, make them straddle the edge of the purple, half-on, half-off. and they
should be spaced such that the last of the three happens at the intersection
point with the circle."

**The curve is redefined.** The constant-curl walk cannot leave radially AND
arrive tangentially. Replace it with two eased curves in polar form: the radius
plateaus into the ring, the sweep starts at zero rate. The flight angle then
falls out as 0 at the root and 90 degrees at the ring — an insertion arc, with
no integration and no normalising division (the tips land on the ring by
construction).

**Constraints that survive:** one parametrisation is the single source of truth;
`rgba()` and `svg()` both derive from it; every shape stays a flat fill;
`assets/yog.svg` regenerated by `make icon`; 100% coverage; 300-line cap.
