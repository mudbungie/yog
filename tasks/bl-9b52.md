## What §16.2 guarantees

> **A wall is born empty, and its rows arrive after birth (bl-00ee).** *yog*
> seeds no wall, ever. A newborn workspace answers brazen's shipped rows and
> nothing else, and every row beyond them — a custom provider entry through the
> §9.1 editor, a sign-in through §8.3's Login roster — is a per-workspace act
> the operator performs afterwards.

Asked of the binary itself through an empty `YOG_WALL` (the same probe
`preflight.sh` uses), a newborn wall's whole table is:

    anthropic         auth api_key  credential missing
    openai            auth bearer   credential missing
    mistral           auth bearer   credential missing
    openai-responses  auth bearer   credential missing
    google            auth api_key  credential missing
    ollama            auth none     credential not required
    claude-code       auth none     credential not required

## The two cases, and neither reaches S0 step 3

**(a) A default install.** `lernie prime` seeds `template/providers.yaml`
naming a row that IS shipped — lernie 0.0.3's template, verbatim:

    roles:
      worker:
        provider: anthropic
        model: claude-sonnet-5
        tools: [bash, dispatch, load_skill, message, read_file]

So the row resolves and the **credential** does not. The first Enter mints the
workspace, mints the conversation, dispatches, and the step dies on auth.

**(b) An operator whose world template names a custom row.** This is a real
configuration, not a hypothetical: a world template's `providers.yaml` names a
provider row that exists only in the host's own brazen config, not among
brazen's shipped rows. Then EVERY workspace raised from that template is born
unable to dispatch at all, with the first step's `response.json` carrying:

    {"type":"error","kind":"config","message":"unknown provider `openai-chatgpt`"}

`kind: config`, not an auth failure — so §8.3's Login roster is the wrong
affordance and the only fix is the §9.1 raw-TOML editor, per workspace, forever.
The provider row and its credential are one fact split across two surfaces.

## Why it is a ruling and not an obvious bug

STORIES.md S0 makes both promises in one story, and only one of them can be
true of a stranger:

> 3. The start renders in flight (busy indicator per step), then **the reply
>    streams into the focused view** … That is the Codex bar: text in, text
>    out, nothing between.

> 5. If the model call needs credentials, the failure surfaces as derived agent
>    state (§13.3 …): the auth-failed step renders with a **Login** affordance
>    one click away, which runs `bz --login --provider <row>` streamed-piped
>    (§8.3 as amended) and paints the device code/URL lines live.

§16.2 decides it: step 5 is what a stranger gets, always. So S0's own headline —
"Stranger: first launch to first reply" — is unreachable in one gesture, and the
same is true of the *second* sphere an operator ever raises, which is the sphere
wall working exactly as ruled. That may be entirely correct and simply
under-described; it may equally be a hole in the first-run experience. The
operator decides.

## The questions

1. **Is the first-turn spend acceptable?** The goal is not preserved through
   this failure. S0 step 4's draft-survives clause is about a *start* that
   aborted before spawning; here the start SUCCEEDED and the driver died after,
   so the operator ends with a conversation, a dead first step, and a goal they
   must retype after signing in. Should a failed first turn be re-dispatchable
   in place (no new conversation, no retype), or is retyping the answer?
2. **Should a config-kind row error get an affordance?** `unknown provider` has
   none today. §9.2's birth gate was the only surface that ever judged the row
   and was retired for judging it against a wall that did not exist yet
   (bl-00ee). Judged against a wall that DOES exist — at the first dispatch, not
   at birth — the same judgement is cheap and has a real remedy to point at.
3. **Should §8.3's Login roster be reachable from the empty state**, before a
   turn is spent, so a stranger can sign the sphere in without burning a
   conversation on the discovery?
4. **Does S0 need a beat for this at all?** Every wire beat in
   `scripts/drive/` now seeds the wall before launch (bl-1851), so the harness
   drives step 3 and has never once driven step 5. Whatever is ruled, the
   *unseeded* first turn deserves a beat — which needs bl-55d8's rendered
   reason to have a surface to assert against.

## Not in scope

The drive harness. bl-1851 delivered the fixture fix; `S0 payoff: wire reply on
disk` is green on a real run and stays green whatever is ruled here.
