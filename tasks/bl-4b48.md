Round 2, lane D2 — implementation child of bl-d13d (the design ball). Its ruling is landed: DESIGN §3.5 "Money everywhere tokens are said, and a ceiling that parks" (the fourth bullet) and §8.6's last bullet, "The spend ceiling is a world floor, and the consult is its second seat". Read those before editing; the docs win over this body. Supersedes bl-06f3 together with its sibling (the price table and the door), which owns the `/ceiling` release — this ball only makes the marks that release finds.

## Premise check (verified on main at the time of filing)

The §8.6 capability control (`src/control/mod.rs`, `judge.rs`) answers `pass`/`hold`/`refuse` per tool invocation, reads `ops.jsonl` under `Consult::state_root`, writes nothing, and never stops a branch. The §4.9 floor (`judge::Answers::floored`, bl-94b4) holds every class above `read` over one descent. `spend::Ceiling::verdict` (`src/spend/ceiling.rs`) is the one at-or-over comparison, `spend::of_world` the world fold, `binding::workspaces` the roster the `Prompt` door already folds over (`src/boundary/ceiling.rs`). `ui.json` lives under `state_root`.

## The deliverable

`Consult` gains the ceiling's verdict, resolved once per consult: open `ui.json` under `state_root` (`UiState::open`), read `ceiling` and `prices`; when BOTH are set, fold `spend::of_world(&binding::workspaces(...), &prices)` and take `Ceiling::verdict(cost)`. When the verdict is `Some(sentence)`, the consult answers `Verdict::Hold(sentence)` for EVERY invocation, every class including `read`, ahead of the table, the floor and the once-answers — except that a standing `/answer pass` for exactly this `tool_use_id` still walks it through (the floor's rule: a once-answer outranks standing policy). When the key is absent or the table empty the read is one `get` and no walk runs: an unbounded or unpriced world costs the consult nothing.

The reason on the mark is `Ceiling::verdict`'s own sentence, unaltered — that is how the sibling's `/ceiling` act finds what the ceiling parked (it matches the fixed head `spend ceiling reached:`) and how the attention queue reads the same words the board's `ceiling` verdict already says.

## What it must not do

- Not stop, not signal, not write: a stop mid-tool-window wedges the branch (litany bl-b98d); a hold costs no process and no tokens; the mark is litany's.
- Not a second comparison: `Ceiling::verdict` is the one function, called from a third seat.
- Not a per-workspace number: the fold is the world's (bl-a80a).
