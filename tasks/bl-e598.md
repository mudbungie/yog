Operator ruling 2026-09-08: the protocol keeps advancing (13 -> 18 in one week, every bump but two ADDITIVE); stabilize around something rational instead of iterating. This ball is the design and yog's half of it; one implementation child per consumer follows.

## The design

1. **`PROTOCOL` is a MAJOR-compatibility number.** It moves only on a breaking change: a field removed or re-typed, a frame's meaning changed under an unchanged spelling, a required field added. It moves 18 -> 19 here, the last bump of the old kind and the first of the new. The hello stays strict equality, fail-closed, no negotiation (REMOTE 3 unchanged).
2. **Additive changes ship without a bump** and are stamped with an **edition**: the old integer line continued (1..18 are editions already). `corpus/shapes.json` stamps every field PATH with the edition it appeared at; the corpus's edition is the newest stamp, computed never stored. `floor` is the edition at which the current major was cut: every path stamped at or below it is required on every engine of that major; every later path is optional to READ.
3. **Readers are grows-only by rule**: unknown key ignored; a post-floor key absent reads as its default; an unknown word in a vocabulary maps to a named catch-all rendered honestly ("unknown state"), never to the nearest known word. The reply `kind` and the request `op` stay strict: a reader asks only what it paints, and the engine refuses what it cannot act on, by name, in band.
4. **Capability discovery replaces the bump for the additive class.** The hello gains `edition` (yog child). A seat that vendors the ledger greys a control whose field's stamp exceeds the engine's edition, and renders an absent post-floor field as *this engine cannot say* rather than as the reassuring default. A consumer's parity ledger records what it does not RENDER, never what it refuses.
5. **The release holds stay, for a MAJOR only** (now rare and deliberate: a design ball with a migration note). The gates need no change: they already fire only when the file moves.
6. **Window rule**: a field or spelling is removed only at a major, only after being listed in `corpus::DEPRECATED` in a PUBLISHED release, and never within 3 yog releases of that listing. The ledger enforces the first two mechanically; the third is the bump ball's checklist.
