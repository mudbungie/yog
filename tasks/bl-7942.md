yog becomes the standalone server: no UI, inert by default. The last step of
REMOTE §12's migration order, and it completes it.

- The eframe/egui/GL stack leaves the crate entirely; **bare `yog` is the
  engine** — what was `yog serve`, minus the verb, because with one face the
  word selects nothing and two spellings of one face are two facts.
- **The window's deprecated release was never cut, deliberately.** §12's order
  gave it one release of overlap while the seat crate was stood up. The
  operator pulled the cutover trigger with every component's publish batched
  into ONE coordinated moment, which makes a deprecation release a publish
  nobody consumes — and the only deployments are the operator's own dev box and
  one server, neither of which needs the overlap. So the window was dropped
  outright rather than deprecated, and `yog seat` / `yog tool-host` went with
  it: both were wire *clients*, and a client is a seat's.
- Ship-inert per bl-37fd is unchanged and verified standing: the tool router is
  total, so a name nobody offers earns an in-band refusal (`tool_host::UNLOADED`)
  and the model steps on. Zero enrolled thralls means no execution surface, and
  that is the posture working.

Deliverable: DESIGN §0 rewritten and §11 retired behind a tombstone carrying a
reading rule; §12's module map re-derived over the tree; REMOTE §8 and §12
amended; the implementation, the gates and the drive harness swept.
