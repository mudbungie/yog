Step 7 of the client/server split, third and last half. bl-4e08 landed the advertisement, the per-client tools.json, connection-scoped presence and the Query::Clients roster. bl-c907 landed the model-facing half: the clients tool (list/get/load) in the stable prefix, the durable per-agent loaded set, and yog's ToolInjection at the lernie Fx seam. What is left is the transport and the far end.

The residual is exact: a loaded remote name is declared to the model, adjudicated by tool-control, and routed — and the routing leg answers a non-zero in-band capture saying this engine carries no leg yet. Everything above it is in place; nothing below it is.

Deliverable:
- The routing leg. An invocation crosses from the driver, through the engine, down the tool host's live connection, and the capture comes back. REMOTE §3 records the frame shape bl-c907 designed: the tool-host connection is the one place the ask direction inverts after establishment, and the design that keeps the existing framing is the long poll — the tool host stays the asker, rides a follow-class read for its next invocation, and posts the capture as an ordinary act. No wire-only verb: whatever the client needs is a boundary verb every face gains (REMOTE §3).
- The engine side: a per-client invocation mailbox beside the presence refcount, and the two boundary reads/acts that drain and answer it. The driver polls for its capture rather than blocking the deposit consumer, which is one thread for the whole world.
- The client-side executor. The tool-host end maps an advertised name to a local command from its own config, and the ADVERTISEMENT is generated from that same config — single source, so what a host offers and what it can run cannot drift. The config shape is recorded in REMOTE §5 by bl-c907; verify it against the tree before building to it.
- A deadline on every routed invocation, and a vanished client as a visible in-band refusal — never a hang. Adjudication is untouched and still runs BEFORE routing.

Honest-scope note carried forward: the executor and the transport may split again if one green-gate ball cannot hold both. Boundary-surface work: serialize.
