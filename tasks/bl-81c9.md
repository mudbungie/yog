The direct embedded command surfaces do not receive the world environment.

The detailed help says verbatim:

`yog bl <argv…>`

`Run balls' own verb surface in this process, against the world's store and landing.`

With `XDG_DATA_HOME=/home/u/data` and `XDG_STATE_HOME=/home/u/ambient`, direct `yog bl prime` and `yog bl create` write beneath `/home/u/ambient/balls`. They do not write beneath the path exported by `yog env`, `/home/u/data/yog/world/state/balls`. The new task is consequently invisible to `/board`.

`yog exec bl ...` uses `/home/u/data/yog/world/state/balls` and is the working workaround.

The same split exists for lernie. Its detailed help says verbatim:

`yog lernie <argv…>`

`Run lernie's own verb surface in this process, against the nested world.`

Direct `yog lernie prime` honors an ambient `LERNIE_HOME`; `yog exec lernie prime` uses `/home/u/data/yog/world/lernie`.

Expected: both advertised embedded surfaces compose the same nested world as every spawned child, or the misleading direct surfaces are removed. One command spelling must not silently operate on a different task and agent universe.
