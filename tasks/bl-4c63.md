rather than `BEADS + 1`, so a phantom sits at the end of the series and is not
drawn. That is what pulls the whole set back off the ring, where the outermost
bead had been eclipsing the rail.

**Ring joints:** measured — the gold is absent over six 6-degree arcs, three of
them the rails butting and three of them beads sitting on top. Fix the first by
running the rail on past the limb's end, along the ring, so consecutive rails
overlap; the overrun disappears under its neighbour. The tail belongs to the
RAIL only — the flesh and the bead layout must not see it, or the beads shift.

the operator to choose; land gold until he picks.
