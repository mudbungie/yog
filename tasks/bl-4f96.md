and judged by this repo's own rule table (`scripts/leak-rules.sh`), the same

examples excluded:

  (bl-2368). Every commit between the two carries it.
- `home-path` — 562 hits over 22 paths: `docs/DESIGN.md` (478),
  `src/opslog/tests.rs` (10), `src/binding/tests.rs` (10),
