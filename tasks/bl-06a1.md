Driving the flagship workflow — an operator starting a conversation whose goal
is "build this small program in this directory" — from the terminal face, every
conversation was fired with the operator's typed goal ALONE. The rung's prefill
never reached the model.

## What DESIGN says

§3.3:

> The path rung appends a target preamble whose first line is the headline —
> `Working directory: <dir>`, the directory verbatim — followed by the
> work-there-by-absolute-path sentence; the ball rung composes the header and
> the ball body verbatim

and, on transparency:

> the operator sees and edits exactly **the whole payload** that is sent —
> their own text and the target/ball prefills, fired verbatim (bl-6920)

## What happens

`/prepare dir <path>` answers with a `prepared` object carrying exactly that
preamble in its `goal` field. `/prompt <goal…>` then passes its own argument
through as the whole payload: `dispatch::doors::prompt` hands `goal` straight
to `start::execute_prompt`, and nothing anywhere concatenates
`prepared.goal` with it. The fired `goal.md` on disk is the operator's text and
nothing else — checked on four conversations.

The prefill is only ever seen by a seat with a composer, which pre-loads the box
with it and sends back the edited whole. A seat without a composer — the
terminal, where each gesture is its own process, which is the face the help page
itself documents the `--prepared` hand-back for — has no way to fire with it
short of pasting it in by hand.

The `/prompt` help page states the opposite of what happens:

> Fires the detached `litany prompt` a `/prepare` made ready, with this goal as
> its whole **tail**, verbatim.

A tail implies a head. There is no head.

## Why it costs something

For the **path rung** the binding still rides `--cwd`, so the work lands in the
right place when a tool resolves the mark. But the sentence the preamble exists
to say — *"Do all work there, by absolute path. Do not rely on the current
directory."* — is exactly the instruction that stops a model writing relative
paths into whatever directory its tool happens to run in. On this drive a
conversation with no preamble did precisely that and its whole deliverable
landed off the target (see the sibling ball on the loaded-tool lane).

For the **ball rung** the loss is total: the prefill IS the payload (`Ball <id>:
<title>` plus the ball body verbatim), so a terminal-fired ball conversation
gets the operator's typed sentence and never sees the ball it is about,
including the `Ball <id>:` headline DESIGN §3.2 calls the conversation→ball
join.

## Shape of the repro

    yog gesture --ws <ws> '/prepare dir <path>'     # reply carries goal: "Working directory: …"
    yog gesture --prepared '<that object>' '/prompt do the thing'
    # read the conversation's goal.md: "do the thing", no preamble

## The question this ball is

Does `/prompt` compose `prepared.goal` + the operator's tail (making the help
page true and the composer's job "edit the composed whole"), or is the prefill
declared composer-only and the help page and §3.3 amended to say so? One of the
two, not a third answer. If it composes, `/fan` takes the same rule.
