REMOTE §8.2 (bl-aaec): `yog seat` and `yog tool-host` resolve the
gesture's workspace name over the entries first; a name no entry holds — and
a gesture naming no workspace — goes to the flat client material exactly as
today (including the `:0` refusal).

Needs bl-5b68 (the entries reader).

What to build:

- **The name rewrite, write direction.** The boundary address tables
  (`src/boundary/address.rs`) are read-only (`Action::workspace()` /
  `Query::workspace()`). Add `pub(crate)` rewrite methods beside them
  (`Action::with_workspace` / `Query::with_workspace`) returning the
  gesture with its workspace field replaced — spent ONLY at the channel
  boundary, when an entry's `workspace` name differs from its leaf (§8.2:
  two directions, one mapping, one place). Not a face capability, not a
  verb (§3's ban untouched — a field edit on an existing gesture).
- `src/wire/seat.rs::open` grows resolution: read the typed gesture's
  workspace name; an entry leaf match opens `Seat::open` on the entry's
  material and rewrites the gesture to the entry's host-side name before
  encoding; otherwise flat `Role::Client` as today. An entry whose
  `channel` is Err refuses with that sentence verbatim, naming the entry.
- `yog tool-host` (`src/tool_host.rs`, `src/wire/host.rs`): serve every
  channel the box is provisioned for — one `Query::Invocations` follow-read
  per entry, plus the flat channel when flat client material exists.
  Execution stays serial PER CHANNEL (REMOTE §10's deferred-concurrency row
  is per-host and untouched). Advertisement presents per channel on that
  channel's own identity.

Line cap: seat.rs is ~140 and host.rs ~140; the per-entry fan-out projects
the host past 200 — pre-split `src/wire/host/entries.rs` per DESIGN §12.

Non-goals: the window (bl-aaec's follow-on balls); no new boundary verbs; no
entry writing.
