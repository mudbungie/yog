Design decision, verified against HEAD and litany 0.0.13 (the pin), before building.

Which of the three dissolves: TWO of them.

(1) The enumeration a seat picks a workflow FROM is already spelled. A mark names a config LINEAGE's head, not a file: `litany workflow <ws> <agent> --config <name>` preflights `require_lineage` and parses that head's `version` and `workflow.yaml`, then writes `refs/litany/workflow/<agent>` at the commit. The harness `workflows/` dir this ball named is litany's TEMPLATE pool (`install.rs` WORKFLOWS_DIR — what `litany new` freezes into a fresh config), which no mark can name; `ConfigFile::LitanyWorkflow` reaches it for editing and that is all it is for. So the read that answers "what can I mark this conversation to?" is `request/lineages` (`Read::Lineages`): every `config/*` lineage with the files its tip holds, `workflow.yaml` among them. No new read; the premise's subject was wrong.

(2) Which workflow governs NOW dissolves into `reply/governing` grown one field, as the ball preferred. `workflow_mark` is `null` when the followed tip's `workflow.yaml` governs (the general path), else `{holder, oid, short_oid, lineage}`: `holder` is the descent id carrying the mark (the agent's own, or the ancestor whose mark it inherits — marking a root switches its tree), `oid` the marked config commit, `lineage` the `config/*` name standing on it or `null` when the lineage has advanced past a mark that pins an older commit. Derived by yog over the same refs litany reads (`nearest_mark`: the agent's id, then each ancestor by the descent grammar), the way `follow.rs` ports `current_config` — never by scraping the verb's one-line stdout, which bl-b95e ruled diagnosis and not a trigger. The commit-side facts (`oid`, `follows`, `files`) still describe the followed tip: under a mark, providers/souls follow the tip and only `workflow.yaml` comes from the marked commit, which is exactly what the field says. An addition, so an EDITION (REMOTE §3.2), stamped by `make corpus`; PROTOCOL stays 19.

(3) The act is new and is the one thing built: `Action::Workflow { workspace, agent, config: Option<String> }`, two ops on the wire (`workflow` with `config` required; `clear-workflow`) on the pin/unpin precedent — the direction is the op token, so a clear can never read as a set that lost its field. `/workflow <lineage>` and `/clear-workflow` on the line, the conversation the seat's as `/retarget`'s is. Executor is `verbs::workflow`, `litany workflow <ws> <agent> --config <name> | --clear`, piped through the workspace's bound wall like every §8.2 verb, answered as `reply/outcome` so an unknown lineage or an unparseable head refuses in litany's own words. An unknown workspace or conversation refuses at the chokepoint's one resolution before anything runs, as every conversation act does.
