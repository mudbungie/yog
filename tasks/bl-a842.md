Found while landing bl-91f1 (the picker's credential remedy), driving the
picker at the paint layer.

## What happens

`src/shell/acceptance/fixture.rs`'s world has a workspace with a transcript, a
settled auth-failed step and an inbox deposit — but **no `config/default`
branch**. So `shell::model_pick::pane` takes its first early return and paints:

    cannot read `roles:` from config/default:providers.yaml — use the Config editors

…and returns. Everything below that line is unreachable from any acceptance
test: the role strip, the two brazen-sourced dropdowns (bl-bd89), the roster
query and its failure paint, the commit-on-select write (bl-fb6b), and the
credential remedy bl-91f1 added.

Verified 2026-08-11 by dumping `Painted` from a four-frame settle with
`world.state.picker.open = true`. The two beats that DO pass on the open picker
— `an_expanded_picker_cannot_grow_the_seat_past_half_the_pane` and
`the_seat_is_bounded_at_the_smallest_supported_window_too` — assert panel
**height**, which grows from the scope sentences and that refusal line alone.
They are true, and they are not about the picker's contents.

## Why it matters

§9.4 is a write surface reached by two seats, and its whole shell half is
coverage-excluded on the stated grounds that *"everything a click calls is
covered"* (tarpaulin.toml). That holds for the judgements. It does not hold for
the wiring, and right now no drive beat can reach any of it.

## The shape of the fix

Seed a `config/default` lineage into the fixture's workspace carrying lernie's
own `template/providers.yaml` — the same text `model_pick::tests`'
`TEMPLATE_PROVIDERS` already pins, so the fixture and the unit tests read one
shape. `crate::git_tree::tests::fixture::Fixture` already authors branches for
this world; this is one more.

Attack it first: the fixture is shared by every acceptance file, so adding a
branch to it can move other beats' geometry. Check whether it belongs on
`world()` or on a `world_with_config()` sibling — and prefer the former if it
holds, since a fixture that cannot render a whole §-owned surface is a hole,
not a variant.

## What it unblocks

A real drive of the §9.4 pane's contents, including both directions of
bl-91f1's gate: that a credential-shaped roster failure paints its remedy and
routes, and that a spawn failure paints no route at all. That second beat was
written and reverted while landing bl-91f1 — it could not get past the refusal
line.
