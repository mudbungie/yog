Found while migrating the drive harness onto the control boundary (bl-e458). VISION §4.8's ruling names three families and puts "the call that populated the contents of the tab" squarely among the QUERIES. DESIGN §8.5's "Staging, stated" paragraph then stages the config family's *actions* — the §9 editors' applies, the §16.3 marks knob, §8.3 login — and bl-3f46 is landing exactly those as Action variants. Nobody has stated where the corresponding READS live.

They are real, on-demand gestures with their own chokepoints and no spelling at any seat:
- the §16.3 marks pane's `Read current` (drives `bl conf` per §16.3; the pane reads on demand, never per frame — DESIGN §11 config-mode bullet: "Opening it is the gesture that reads everything the pane renders (§9.5)");
- the §9 editors' `Reload` (re-diffs the destination — the hash-guard's second half, §9.1/§9.4);
- the §8.3 login pane's `↻ providers + credentials`.

`boundary::Query` today is Workspaces / Conversations / Balls / Ops / Search / Help — no marks, no providers, no config. So a headless seat can (after bl-3f46) SET the marks knob and cannot ASK what it is; it can apply a brazen config and cannot read the one on disk. That asymmetry is the thing to rule on.

Deliverable is a ruling in DESIGN §8.5 first, code second. Either these are queries and join `Query` beside Balls/Ops (with the codec + line + help rows the compile gate demands), or §8.5 states why a read whose subject is a *tool's own configuration* sits outside the taxonomy — but it must not stay unsaid, because §8.5's compile gate only bites on gestures that are already variants.

Evidence in tree: scripts/drive/beats_s8.sh's `Read current` click is tagged NO SPELLING AT ALL and cites this ball; scripts/drive/beats_s5.sh's Apply/Reload clicks are tagged NO SPELLING YET and cite bl-3f46. Verify §8.5 and the `Query` enum on main before editing — bl-3f46 may have moved both.
