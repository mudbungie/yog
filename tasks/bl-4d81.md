DESIGN §7.3 ("Failed action", "How a banner ends", "Which surface is the
originating surface"), §4.2, §6. Found by the §11-rule sweep (bl-7dca).

§7.3 rules that a failed action is **a stated fact, never stderr-only, and
stated exactly once**, at the one surface the op's `origin` names; that a
banner ends two ways (retirement by a newer clean op of the same origin, or the
operator's `ack-failures` watermark); and that a driver *notice* is not a
failure (bl-1296). Every one of those is a derivation this crate owns and none
of them crosses.

`Query::Ops` answers `snap.ops`'s last `max` rows encoded as
`{ts, argv, cwd, exit, stdout, stderr, origin}` (`boundary::reply::rows::op_row`).
Not carried: `OpRow::failed`, `OpRow::drift`, `ExitKind`/`exit_label`,
`OpOutcome` and the retirement projection (`opslog::live::outcomes`), the
notice classifier, and the `since_ack` slice. `AppModel::last_failure` and
`SurfaceFailure` (`src/app/balls.rs`) — the one home of *which failure is live
at which surface* — have **no production caller** since bl-7942.

So a seat that wants the banner §7.3 mandates must re-implement: the sentinel
reading (`-1`/`-2`/`-3`/`-4` and `128+n`), the bl-1296 notice phrase table, the
`(cwd, verb)` retirement key with `verb` as the leading two argv tokens, and the
ack watermark scan. That is five derivations duplicated per seat, each of which
this document names a single home for — and the failure mode is silent
divergence, not a compile error.

Two things are already right and should not move: `origin` is **stored** on the
row precisely because it cannot be derived (bl-48f8), and it does cross. And
the ack is a durable `ops.jsonl` line (bl-c417), so it converges. What is
missing is only the reading.

Candidate shapes: the ops reply carries the derived fields per row
(`failed`, `outcome`, `exit_label`, `acked`) so the seat renders rather than
classifies; or a `Query::Failures` answering `last_failure` per origin
directly, which is the shape §7.3 actually describes ("one banner surface per
subject") and the one that keeps `SurfaceFailure` alive; or both, since the
row-level fields and the per-origin standing answer are different questions.

Amend §7.3's three rows and §4.2's badge paragraph to name the carrier when it
lands, and decide `AppModel::last_failure`/`SurfaceFailure` with it (bl-7cc8 is
the ball for the wider seat-residue class).
