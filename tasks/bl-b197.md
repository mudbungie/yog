The mark ships with a gold rail (`deep(BRAZEN, GILT)`). Three alternates were
rendered and rejected for now; record them so the exploration is not lost with
the scratchpad, and so the next person does not re-run it.

**the operator's ruling:** "Keep the alt colors around as potentials, but ship the gold
version."

Record: the swap is one constant, the four candidates and their derived hexes,
and *why* two of them fail — blue and magenta sit too close to the flesh's
violet in hue, so the rail stops reading as its own element and the ring muddies
into the limb at icon sizes. Gold and red hold separation; red reads as an
alarm, gold as a highlight.
