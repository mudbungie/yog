Found by bl-d147. Until bl-d147 every yog coverage run used tarpaulin's ptrace engine: tarpaulin 0.35.2 ignores a command-line --engine whenever tarpaulin.toml exists, so the Makefile's --engine llvm never took effect. Yet a dozen comments and rules attribute phantom uncovered regions to llvm-cov, and several structural rulings rest on that: the four code carve-outs of rules/locks-outside-state.yml (AGENTS rule 7: probe_cache, fs_watcher hub, registry presence, registry mailbox slots), the state.rs spelling rule in DESIGN §12's src/state.rs row, the 'bound rather than chained' splits in src/fork/choices.rs and src/git_tree/tests/state_unit.rs, and the impl-header notes in src/lib.rs, src/app/mod.rs, src/app/boot.rs, src/binding/mod.rs, src/registry.rs. The phantoms were most likely ptrace's DWARF path collision (a dependency's relative src/<name>.rs resolved to ours). Work: for each, try the folded or chained form under the llvm engine on the builder (bl-remote-run coverage); where the phantom does not reappear, undo the workaround and the carve-out; where it does, correct the attribution text. Either way no comment may keep naming llvm-cov for a measurement ptrace made.
