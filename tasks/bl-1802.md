Operator complaint, 2026-08-05: *"the history is riding alongside the chat --
that's good, but it's its own window. Rather, every operable commit should be a
horizontal rule across the chat, instead of a window on the side. the fork
overlay should show up when you click on one."*

## The observation behind it

The same fact is drawn twice.

- `src/transcript/rows/crossings.rs` — *"Every crossing leaves its line"*: one
  horizontal rule per commit boundary in the chat, *"the **id** is the ensuing
  step's `meta.json` `commit`"*. Today the rule is inert: it marks, it does not
  operate.
- `src/rail/mod.rs` — *"One **notch** per step, each notch that step's
  read-state commit — the branch tip the model call was assembled against,
  already recorded in `meta.json`"*, rendered as `egui::SidePanel::left("history-rail")`
  at `src/inspector/mod.rs:128`. The notch carries the pin and *Fork from here*.

Same commit, same source (`meta.json`), two renderings, one of which costs a
window. The ruling: **the rule IS the notch.** The side panel goes away; the
gesture moves onto the line in the chat, and clicking a rule raises the fork
overlay (`src/fork/`).

## The hole this opens, and how to fill it

`src/inspector/mod.rs:116`, verbatim:

> **The rail is the inspector's gutter, not the Transcript tab's** — the pin
> reaches all four pinnable tabs (VISION V1.2), so its control has to be
> reachable from all four; a rail that lived inside one tab would strand an
> operator who pinned and then switched.

That is a real objection and it must be answered in the design, not stepped
over. The pin still reaches Files/Config/Budget; only its *control* moves into
the Transcript tab. So the operator who pins, switches to Files, and wants out
has no notch to click.

The fill is the banner that already exists — `pinned_banner`, which paints on
every pinnable tab and currently says *"Pick the same mark again to come
back."* That sentence stops being true the moment the mark lives in another
tab. The banner has to carry the release itself. Decide and record: is the
release a control on the banner, or does the banner just route back to the
Transcript tab? Prefer the answer that adds no new verb.

Also settle, before writing code:

- **Which commits are operable.** The complaint says "every operable commit".
  Crossings draw only where the ensuing step landed a `meta.json` commit
  (*"Absence of a commit = no line"*), and the rail's notches are the same set
  — confirm they are literally the same set, and if they are not, that
  divergence is the actual bug to fix first.
- **What the rail rendered that the rule cannot.** `src/rail/` also carries
  cards, cohorts (V2's ×N), and the two-edge context/provenance distinction
  (VISION V1.3, solid vs dashed). A rule across a chat cannot draw a dashed
  provenance edge to another agent. Say explicitly what survives, where it
  goes, and what is deliberately dropped — do **not** silently delete V1.3.
  If the descent/edge drawing needs a home that is not the chat, name it and
  file a follow-up ball rather than smuggling it in.
- **Docs first.** `docs/VISION.md` V1 (the Historian rung) and `docs/DESIGN.md`
  §11 both describe the rail as a gutter. This ball's deliverable includes
  those edits — *"don't implement a deviation: fix the doc."*
