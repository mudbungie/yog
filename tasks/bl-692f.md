Rework the icon landed by bl-76f6 (DESIGN §11).

**the operator's spec, verbatim:** "I'd like things to align better, and geometry a
little more. So, let's do this. First, drop the three dimensional/shading on the
components. It's not coming through. Circle in the middle. Green, electric
green. Purple tentacles coming off it, with a gold highlight on the outside of
the curve, and on the inside, a series of three more circles, smaller than the
middle one. The tentacles themselves reduce in width until they all intersect
at the same circle on the outside."

**Consequences:** no lit spheres, no gradients, no light model — every shape is
a flat fill, which collapses the rasterizer and makes the two renderings
identical by construction rather than merely faithful. The walk is normalised
so every limb's tip node lands on one outer ring (the alignment). Palette is
three derived colours: deep(HYDRA) driven electric, deep(GATE) purple,
deep(BRAZEN) gold.

**Constraints that survive:** one walk is the single source of truth; `rgba()`
and `svg()` both derive from it; `assets/yog.svg` regenerated by `make icon`;
transparency; 100% coverage; 300-line cap.
