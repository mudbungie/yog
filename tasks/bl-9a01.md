Glyph-doctrine follow-up (DESIGN §11 "Glyph doctrine", filed by bl-5013).

src/shell/navigator.rs (workspace_tab): a replay workspace hoisted into the tab
row is marked only by a " ▶" suffix. ▶ conventionally reads "play/start", not
"replay/read-only", so it fails the "extremely clear" bar; delete it and
nothing on the tab distinguishes a replay regime from a live one.

CORRECTED 2026-07-29 (the original clause "the word is lost exactly when the
entry is pinned" is stale since bl-7e32/51939f8): pinning no longer hoists an
entry OUT of the overflow menu — `nav::tabs::build` leaves every foreign/replay
entry listed there, pinned or not, so its worded `· replay` row survives. The
fail stands on the narrower ground the amended §11 audit row states: the word is
one click behind the ⋯, not co-visible with the tab. (The center's
"replay · read-only" line likewise appears only once focused.)

Fix per the doctrine: say it on the tab — hover text at minimum
("replay — read-only"), or keep the "· replay" word from the overflow entry.
