Glyph-doctrine follow-up (DESIGN §11 "Glyph doctrine", filed by bl-5013).

src/shell/navigator.rs (workspace_tab): a replay workspace hoisted into the tab
row is marked only by a " ▶" suffix. ▶ conventionally reads "play/start", not
"replay/read-only", so it fails the "extremely clear" bar; delete it and
nothing on the tab distinguishes a replay regime from a live one. (The overflow
menu says "replay" in words; the word is lost exactly when the entry is pinned.
The center's "replay · read-only" line appears only once focused.)

Fix per the doctrine: say it on the tab — hover text at minimum ("replay —
read-only"), or keep the "· replay" word from the overflow entry.
