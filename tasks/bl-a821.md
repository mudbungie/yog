    $ lernie --json revoke ops NoodleGarden

    held: alpha2_Bash — alpha2_Bash {"command":"echo -n hello > /srv/marker.txt
      && cat /srv/marker.txt"} classified open-world (redirects output to
      /srv/marker.txt, outside the writable root)

No file was created. Good.

## The side effect

`revoke` is documented as taking auto-approval away from "this conversation and
its descendants", and the compactor is a descendant. So the floor caught the
compaction machinery:

    ops/PorchSandal  quiescent  5s  held
      held: write_summary (toolu_01…) — write_summary {"content":"# alpha2 System
      Status Report\n\n## Dispatch Goal: System Diagnostics\n\nRan four
      diagnostic commands on alpha2 …"} classified target write (writes the
      conversation's own summary onto the compactor branch)

`PorchSandal` is the compactor conversation for the floored root. Its
`write_summary` is machinery: the pair is injected by the compaction procedure
and, in litany's own shipped `providers.yaml`, "is never declarable here". The
operator is being asked to adjudicate an act they have no basis to judge and
did not ask for, and until they answer it the floored conversation cannot
compact — so a long conversation under a floor will eventually stall on
context rather than on policy.

## The distinction the floor is missing

A floor is a statement about what the AGENT may do to the world. The compactor's
summary write touches the conversation's own compactor branch and nothing else;
it is not an act on any box, in any workspace, that an operator would ever want
to gate. The same argument bl-52b7 made for the compactor's grant — machinery is
confined by construction, not by adjudication — applies to what a floor reaches.

## Ask

A floor does not reach the checkpoint pair (`write_summary` /
`mark_for_deletion`), the two tools no role can declare. Everything else about
descendant propagation stays: a dispatched child agent's calls are the agent's
acts and the floor should reach them.

p3: it does not lose data and the operator can pass it, but it puts a decision
in the queue that has no decision in it, and it stalls compaction under exactly
the policy an operator sets when they are most worried.
