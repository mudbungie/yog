Round-1 multitenant lane, REMOTE §4.1 / §5.

## Gesture and reply

Three enrollments were minted during setup and never used — a mistyped name, a
second attempt, an experiment. Every seat in the workspace now reads:

    $ lernie clients alpha
    {"client":"phone-emu",  "present":false,"tools":[]}
    {"client":"phone-emu2", "present":false,"tools":[]}
    {"client":"phone-emu3", "present":false,"tools":[]}
    {"client":"phone-seat", "present":true, "tools":[…16 tools…]}
    {"client":"seat1",      "present":true, "tools":[]}
    {"client":"seat2",      "present":false,"tools":[]}

`phone-emu`, `phone-emu2` and `phone-emu3` never connected once. `seat2` is a
live desktop seat that spoke ten seconds ago. They are the same row.

## Why the design's own answer is not quite enough

§4.1 rules that no gesture manages registrations, and that is right — the
management surface is the operator's own `mkdir`/`touch`/`rm` and the read side
is §5's client tool. This ball is entirely about the READ side, which §4.1
leaves to §5.

Two facts exist today: durable advertisement, and live presence. Neither can
distinguish "never once connected" from "connected constantly and is asleep
right now", and on a CLI seat presence is `false` for everything anyway because
every verb opens and closes its own connection — presence reads `true` only for
a seat holding `follow`. So on a workspace driven from a terminal, the roster is
a list of names and nothing else.

## Expected

One more durable fact on the registration, changing at the rate of a
connection rather than of a blip: **last seen**, or its absence for a client
that has never connected. It is a per-client fact the engine already observes
at the one place it accepts a leaf, and it turns the roster from a list of names
into an answer to "is that machine real".

## Severity

p3. Nothing is broken; the cost is that the roster stops being trustworthy on a
long-lived engine, which is precisely the engine this design is for. It also
makes the operator's `rm` remedy unusable in practice, because you cannot tell
which rows are safe to delete.
