Found on the first real 0.0.6 deploy. scripts/deploy/seat.sh retags locally and carries the image with save | load, but podman's archive names the image localhost/yog:<tag>; docker load restores that name, and the unit's YOG_IMAGE says yog:<tag> — ExecStart cannot resolve it, the unit crash-loops under Restart=always, and the script's final status block prints an 'active (running)' unit because active means the docker CLI process exists, not that the engine serves. Remedied by hand this once (docker tag on the box, restart, wire answered). Two fixes, both wanted: (1) retag on the box after the load (or write the localhost/-prefixed name into deploy.env — pick one spelling and record why); (2) the script's last act becomes a VERIFICATION, not a status print — a workspaces query over the real wire from the deploying box, failing the script when the engine does not answer. A deploy that cannot tell success from a crash loop is the reconciler's blindness reinvented at the moment it acts.
