Round-1 multitenant lane, REMOTE §8.1 start flow.

## Gesture and reply

    $ lernie start alpha "Say hello and stop."
    {"kind":"prepared","ok":true,…}
    {"conversation":"VelvetCordial","kind":"started","ok":true}
    $ lernie follow alpha VelvetCordial
    {"error":"unknown conversation \"VelvetCordial\"","ok":false}

Reproduced 2/2 on two consecutive starts with different names. A one-second
poll on `transcript` measured the window: refused on try 1, answered on try 2,
so roughly 1–2 seconds.

The first sighting of this was worse to read: a `follow` fired immediately
after a `start` printed **one empty line** and exited 1 with nothing on stderr,
which reads as a wire fault rather than a name that is not there yet.

## Why it matters here

This lane is two seats on one engine. The whole handoff is "I started
`VelvetCordial`, go look at it" — and the second seat is refused with the same
sentence a workspace that never existed earns. It is also every hand-typed
`start` followed by `follow`, and every script that does the obvious thing with
the name `start` just returned.

## Expected

The name `start` answers with is addressable by the time it is answered. The
`started` reply is the engine's own statement that the conversation exists; a
read that names it must not then say it does not.

## Severity

p2. Not a data defect and it self-heals in a second, but it lands on the one
gesture pair every operator types first, and its refusal is
indistinguishable from the refusal for a name that is genuinely wrong.
