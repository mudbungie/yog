The engine's rendezvous loop (src/wire/rendezvous/cycle.rs, REMOTE §13.4) publishes presence hourly, polls the inbox every 15 s, opens calls and punches, and writes nothing to the engine's log at any point; its Stats counters reach no wire reply. Observed: an engine that walks the DHT fine, a seat's call filed in the inbox, and no punch, with nothing on the engine able to say whether the item was read, verified, opened, what nonce was seen, or whether a punch was attempted and toward how many endpoints.

Deliver: (1) one stderr line per loop event in the house `yog: rendezvous: …` shape — loop started, presence published or failed, each non-quiet poll outcome (did not verify / did not open / nonce already seen / call opened → punch started), punch landed or expired, served stream ended — counts, seqs, nonces and address families only, never an address, key, salt or sealed byte. (2) A `rendezvous` object on reply/doctor: {active, published, calls, punched, served, last_poll_unix}, an edition, no PROTOCOL bump. (3) Tests for every log arm through the bench with an injected sink. Amend DESIGN §12 and REMOTE §13.4.
