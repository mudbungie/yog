The seat repo's bl-b180 (re-homed from this board's bl-fef7) is the bl-1fd0
ruling with no home: on a wall holding no usable provider credential, typing a
goal and pressing Enter works zero percent of the time, and since bl-7cc8
deleted the callerless `StartGate`/`WallCredit` fold the predicate is stated in
DESIGN §8.1 and enforced nowhere. §8.1's own closing line reserved this ball:
*"If a seat ever needs yog to state the gate, it files against yog and the
carrier is designed then."*

## Ruling (designed on the seat's bl-b180, built here)

**The engine decides, at the `Prompt` door, and the carrier is the refusal it
already has.** Three reasons, each a house principle rather than a preference:

- One home. A seat that re-judged `Query::Providers` rows would be a second
  implementation of a settled decision, and there are two seats now (the
  desktop window and the phone), so it would be a third. The door is the one
  place every spelling of the fire passes — a click, a line, a deposit — which
  is exactly why bl-54c1 seated the blank-goal refusal there.
- No new verb. A refused fire is `{"ok": false, "error": …}`, the envelope
  every act already answers with; the seat paints it where it paints every
  refusal. `PROTOCOL` stands, the corpus is unchanged, no shape moves.
- The waiting question dissolves. A pre-read the seat must hold before it
  offers a first fire has a race (a goal typed before the answer is back);
  a door has none — the goal is posted as always and the answer is ready or
  refused, and a refusal spends nothing: the goal comes back with the sentence.

**The predicate is §8.1's, plus one clause the door's placement forces.** A
wall is ready when any row of its effective table is `credentialed()` — every
spelling but `missing` and `not required`, brazen's own column. A keyless row
does not ready the wall (the built-in table merges under every config, so a
predicate it satisfied would be one nothing fails) — **unless the lineage the
fire forks off names it in a role.** A role pointing at `ollama` is the
operator's own hand, not the merge, and a gate on the engine has no
read-past: the seat's sentence could be ignored, a door's refusal cannot, so
the conservatism §8.1 recorded as tolerable becomes a keyless-only setup that
can never start a conversation. The clause is one read of the file §9.4
already reads at its own gate.

**Order at the door**: blank goal → **sign in** → confinement → ceiling. The
table read is in-process brazen over the wall (no spawn, no network); it
stands ahead of the ceiling so a refused fire writes no trail row, and behind
the blank goal so a fire with nothing to say reads no wall.
