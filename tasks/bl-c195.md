Follow-up to bl-a75f, per operator feedback:

1. Identity is stamped by the harness, not the model: workspace-scoped spawns carry YOG_NAME=<name>; the W9 bl agent-tool shim injects --as $YOG_NAME when the caller omits it. The preamble 'Stamp every bl verb' instruction is phase-1 interim, deleted with W9.
2. Workspace granularity: a workspace is a long-lived sphere wall (personal / corporate / client), not a per-conversation ephemeral. Prompts are new roots in the focused workspace; a world with zero workspaces mints one (bootstrap = the empty general case); + New workspace is the rare deliberate verb. Start ladder becomes two axes: target workspace x payload (bare / path / ball).
