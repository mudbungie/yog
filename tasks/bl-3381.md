## Decisions (2026-08-01, operator)

1. **lernie becomes the clock.** Watcher-cycle scheduling moves into the harness; cron exits the design. Filed upstream as **lernie bl-36a2** (design + implementation of the clock, incl. amending ARCH §8's 'no scheduler by design'). yog consumes the clock's derivable state ('last wake / next wake') on the operator surface rather than owning any timers itself — consistent with the bl-ee0a principle (UI totally isolated from backend operations).
